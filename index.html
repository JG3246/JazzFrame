JazzEra
JazzEra has only one agenda – to promote the enjoyment of jazz.

Jazz lives, in a few places it even thrives, but for lots of people jazz is no more than a word, it never becomes a sound. JazzEra wants to change that. We want to make jazz available to anyone interested, but more than that, we want to give listeners a chance to respond, to get involved. We do this by marrying the music to the technology. If you have a phone you can be part of JazzEra.

Jazz never developed anything equivalent to the pop video scene. Quite why that was, no one knows. Maybe because as the interest in tech rose the interest in jazz declined. Anyway, whatever the reason it never happened. Now it can – and will.

JazzEra is a site where the music is provided and the user does the rest. Having listened to the track the user can respond with a video — one they create to go with their own interpretation of the track, or one just to see how it works. If they or a friend can write lyrics, they can create a sing-along video, and then, if they like what they’ve done, they can share.

There are currently fifty-three tracks in the JazzEra portfolio — one with a video made to show what’s possible, and fifty-two waiting.

JazzEra is not a commercial venture, it’s free and we’re keen to share. The tracks at present are all AI generated because the developer is definitely not musical. He’s a listener not a performer. JazzEra, now it’s live, is open to anyone — musicians, filmmakers, researchers, developers — we’d like anyone who enjoys jazz and who wants to take the idea further to be able to participate.

At the moment we’re testing the site so it’s invite-only to access and use. If you provide your email it will be added to the invited list and you will be able to access the Homepage and explore. If you think this might be for you then click here to go to the landing site…and enjoy.
